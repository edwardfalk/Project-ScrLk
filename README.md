# Project ScrLk

The AI-assistant Happy Mac lives in a Macintosh Classic from 1990. You talk to him through a cobra phone from 1956. Happy Mac controls the apps and games in the simulated OS environment and gives you tips about where to eat lunch. Happy Mac's favorite artists are Floyd-Steinberg.

## Origin

During our electronic engineering studies at Yrgo we were interns at EQ2 in Gothenburg, Sweden. On the first day there our supervisor Johannes gave us the monochrome Macintosh Classic and the Ericophon and said: "I want to be able to talk to that computer through this phone".
